YUME

YUME is a Ukrainian tea brand founded in 2019 in Odesa. Its matcha, Japanese and Chinese teas, oolongs, pu-erhs and signature blends are served in more than 145 cafés and restaurants across Ukraine. The team's goal is to introduce tea lovers to real tea at a fair price.
