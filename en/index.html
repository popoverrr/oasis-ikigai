The philosophy

The Art of Ikigai

Ikigai is often described as the reason to get up in the morning — the balance between what gives us energy, meaning and wellbeing.

At OASIS IKIGAI we translate that philosophy into small daily wellness rituals: a short, considered routine of Japanese formulas, used consistently rather than intensively.

日本Standards

Why Japanese quality is trusted

  1. 01The state checks health claims

    Since 1991 Japan has run the state FOSHU system: a product may claim a health benefit only after its scientific evidence has been reviewed.

  2. 02Every claim is backed by evidence

    Since 2015, makers of functional foods must file their scientific evidence with Japan’s Consumer Affairs Agency before sales begin, and the filings are public.

  3. 03A culture of daily self-care

    Looking after your health every day is a habit in Japan, and its life expectancy is among the highest in the world.

You order the original

Only the original product guarantees the stated ingredients, dosage and proper storage — and with them the effect you choose it for. Directly from Japanese makers and suppliers.
