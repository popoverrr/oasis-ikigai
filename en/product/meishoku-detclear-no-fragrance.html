Ask about this product

Good for

美Beauty

Who is it for and how does it work?

The gel spreads in a thin layer and, after about a minute of gentle massage, forms little rolls that pick up dead skin cells and impurities.

  • Fruit AHAs and BHA gently exfoliate and even out skin tone.
  • Help clear pores and control excess sebum.
  • Leave skin smooth and help the next skincare steps absorb better.
  • A fragrance-free version with lower acidity for sensitive skin.

Who it is for

For all skin types — combination, oily-prone, normal and sensitive.

  • Sensitive, reactive skin: a formula free from fragrance, dyes, alcohol, parabens and mineral oils works very gently.
  • Dull, uneven skin: fruit AHAs gently lift dead skin cells and bring back glow and a fresh tone.
  • Enlarged pores: plant BHAs dissolve excess sebum and help clear pores and blackheads in the T-zone.
  • Rough skin: the jelly rolls away dead cells as you massage, leaving skin smooth so serums and creams absorb better.

Key ingredients

  • AHA (alpha hydroxy acids)Tartaric and malic acids gently exfoliate and smooth the skin.
  • BHA (salicylic acid)Penetrates pores and helps clear them.
  • Placenta and dipotassium glycyrrhizateWater-soluble placenta extract and a soothing liquorice-derived ingredient.
  • Sodium hyaluronate and collagen tripeptideHelp keep the skin comfortable and hydrated.

How to take it and what's inside?

Apply a thin layer to clean, dry skin and massage for 1–⁠2 minutes, focusing on problem areas, until rolls appear. Rinse with warm water. For sensitive skin, start once a week.

For external use only. Do not apply to damaged skin. Stop using if redness, itching or irritation occur.

Ingredients

Active ingredients: water-soluble placenta extract, dipotassium glycyrrhizate.

Other ingredients: evening primrose extract, Alpinia katsumadai seed extract, sodium hyaluronate‑2, collagen tripeptide F, stearoyl phytosphingosine, tartaric acid, raspberry extract, salicylic acid, tri-POE(5) stearyl ammonium chloride, BG, DL-malic acid, hydroxyproline, plant extract blend‑34, concentrated glycerin, coix seed extract, apple extract, peach leaf extract, carbomer, acrylates/alkyl methacrylate copolymer, phenoxyethanol, cetrimonium chloride.

Free from fragrance, artificial colourings, mineral oil, alcohol and parabens.
