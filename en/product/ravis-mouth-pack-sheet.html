Who is it for and how does it work?

RAViS Mouth Pack Sheet are overnight patches for the smile-line area.

  • Intense hydration: actives prevent dryness, leaving skin soft and radiant.
  • Anti-ageing action: smooth creases, support collagen and elastin and improve firmness.
  • Comfortable overnight: a soft, delicate material that stays close to the skin.

Who it is for

For women and men who want to smooth their smile lines.

  • Age 25–⁠30+: when expression lines and nasolabial creases appear and skin loses density.
  • Skin type: dry, normal and combination skin that needs deep hydration and a lifting effect.

Key ingredients

  • Retinol (retinyl palmitate)Smooths lines, evens tone, supports collagen and elastin.
  • Vitamin ESupports cell renewal and skin elasticity.
  • Coenzyme Q10An antioxidant that supports firmness.
  • Hyaluronic acidA powerful humectant.

How to take it and what's inside?

Before bed, apply the patches to clean, dry skin along the smile lines, leave overnight and remove in the morning.

Ingredients

Acrylates/VP copolymer, isopropyl myristate, tocopheryl acetate, retinyl palmitate, ubiquinone (Q10), sodium hyaluronate, pyridoxine tris-hexyldecanoate, peanut oil.
